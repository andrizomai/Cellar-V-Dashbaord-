# Recording & submission checklist

Pulled directly from the assignment's requirements — check every box before you submit.

## Recording requirements
- [ ] **Single video file** — screen recording (dashboard walkthrough) *with your
      webcam visible the whole time*, not a slide deck with per-slide clips.
- [ ] **5–10 minutes** total length.
- [ ] **State your name** out loud near the start.
- [ ] **Hold a government-issued ID up to the camera** — name and photo clearly
      legible.
- [ ] **Captions or a transcript** included. Easiest path: record in **Loom**,
      **Zoom**, or **Google Meet** (upload the Meet recording to YouTube as
      *unlisted* to get auto-captions), all of which can auto-generate captions.
- [ ] Every group member on camera at some point, if this isn't a solo submission
      (you're doing this solo based on our conversation, so just you).

## Suggested recording tool
Since you're on a Mac and want screen + webcam + auto-captions with minimal
setup: **Loom** is the fastest (free tier covers this length, auto-captions
included, gives you a shareable link immediately — though the assignment
wants a **Google Drive** link specifically, so you'd still need to download
the Loom file and re-upload to Drive). Alternatively, **Zoom** (record a
solo meeting with screen share, auto-transcript available on paid plans) or
**QuickTime screen recording + separate webcam overlay** if you want full
control, though that skips auto-captions (you'd need to add a transcript
manually or run it through Descript/YouTube captions afterward).

## File hosting
- [ ] Upload the final video to **Google Drive** (the assignment explicitly
      requests this over other file-transfer services).
- [ ] Set sharing to **"Anyone with the link can view"** — do **not** use
      Drive's "Invite people" — the graders don't share one account, so a
      named invite won't reach them.
- [ ] Confirm the link doesn't expire in under 5 days.

## The PDF you also submit
- [ ] Your slide deck (PowerPoint/Keynote/Google Slides), exported to PDF.
- [ ] A link to your **deployed Streamlit app** (preferred) or the **public
      GitHub repo** — both must be reachable with **no login required**.
- [ ] If you go the GitHub-repo route: keep the repo **public** through the
      entire grading window (3–4 weeks after your submission date).

## Submit
- [ ] Click **"Submit Project"** on your Quantic dashboard and follow the
      prompts — you'll paste in both the video link and attach/link the PDF.
- [ ] Questions go to **projects+msba@quantic.edu**.
